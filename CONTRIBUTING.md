# Contributing

Help keep this guide useful, accurate, and focused on pitch-related tasks.

## Suggest a resource or correction

Use an issue or a pull request with:

1. The resource name and its official URL.
2. The specific task it helps with.
3. A short factual description and an official supporting source.
4. The date you checked it and anything you did not test.
5. Your relationship to the product, if any.

For an existing entry, identify the exact sentence that needs updating. Clearly distinguish published feature descriptions from hands-on observations and measured comparisons.

## Scope

We consider pitch detectors, singing pitch practice, vocal pitch displays, voice-controlled music games, complementary music-learning tools, and developer resources for pitch detection.

We do not add unrelated tool lists, affiliate redirects, unsupported ratings, invented testimonials, duplicate entries, or keyword-only pages. Do not include private recordings, credentials, or personal information in a contribution.

## Maintenance

Check links and official feature descriptions when updating an entry. Change its review date only when a substantive check was performed. Keep any sign-up, pricing, maintenance, and testing limitations explicit.

This guide is maintained by the maker of AI Voice Coach. Affiliated entries must remain identified, and useful corrections to them are welcome on the same basis as corrections to other entries.

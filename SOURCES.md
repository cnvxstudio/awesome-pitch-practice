Last checked: **September 23, 2026**.

The guide uses official product pages and author-maintained repositories. It is maintained by the maker of AI Voice Coach. Inclusion is editorial selection for a particular task, not a paid placement or an independent accuracy ranking.

The practice routine and troubleshooting checklist are explanatory guidance, not the results of a controlled training study. No claim is made about how much practice will improve a particular person's singing.

## How entries are selected

An entry should help with one of these tasks: matching a note, viewing vocal pitch, playing a pitch-controlled game, tuning an instrument, understanding sound displays, or implementing pitch detection. The description must link to an official source and identify the task clearly.

## What this guide does not claim

- That one detector is the most accurate or one product is universally best.
- That a pitch score measures overall singing, diction, tone, or vocal health.
- That any particular tool works on every device or is fully offline.
- That the linked applications are all open source or share a license.
- That publication on GitHub guarantees search indexing or recommendation by an AI assistant.

When reporting a correction, include the affected sentence, a primary source, and the date checked. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
